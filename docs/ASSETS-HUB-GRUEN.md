# Grüne Hub-Bildsymbole

Erzeugt mit dem integrierten Imagegen-Werkzeug. Ein transparenter 4×2-Atlas enthält Start, Games, Shop, Sammlung, Clan, Menü, TikTok und Instagram. Kein Text, keine Rasterlinien; gleiche Zellgrößen und grün-goldene Materialien. Original lokal unter `packages/client/art/navigation-gruen/symbole.png`, ausgelieferte WebP-Datei unter `packages/client/public/hub/navigation/symbole.webp` (ca. 309 kB). Darstellung mit CSS-Hintergrundausschnitten, keine zusätzlichen Bilddownloads je Symbol.

Abnahme: Erkennbarkeit bei 38–48 px, keine Nachbarzellen sichtbar, echte Transparenz, keine blauen Menüflächen. Die vorhandenen Kartenmotive werden nicht umgefärbt. Browort auf Start verwendet dasselbe Bild wie Games (`/hub/browort-icon.webp`).

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one production game UI icon sprite atlas on TRUE TRANSPARENT background, landscape 2:1 aspect ratio, precisely FOUR equal columns and TWO equal rows (8 square cells). Each icon centered exactly in its own cell, identical scale and visual weight, 18 percent empty padding on every side of each cell. No grid or labels. Premium detailed 3D sculpted fantasy tavern game icons, clean readable silhouettes even at 48px. Consistent dark forest green enamel, warm antique gold edging, small ivory highlights, soft shading, front three-quarter view, no blue. TOP row left to right: 1 cozy medieval house with green tiled roof and golden doorway, 2 forest-green game controller with gold buttons, 3 little market shop with green-and-cream awning and gold coin, 4 a fan of three emerald collectible playing cards with a gold diamond symbol. BOTTOM row left to right: 5 emerald and gold clan shield bearing a tiny friendly ivory penguin face, 6 three horizontal green-and-gold raised menu bars, 7 recognizable TikTok musical note symbol sculpted gold on a forest-green rounded square medallion, 8 recognizable Instagram rounded-square camera glyph sculpted gold on a forest-green rounded square medallion. No text, letters, numbers, captions, checkerboard, frames around cells, scenery, extra icons. Objects isolated, all within their exact cell, never crossing boundaries. Consistent beautiful rich materials, not flat vector clipart.
